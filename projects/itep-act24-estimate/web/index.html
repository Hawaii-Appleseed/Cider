What ITEP’s $622M means

ITEP estimates that by 2031, even after Act 24, Hawaiʻi will collect $622 million a year less in income tax than it would under 2026 law. That counts only the part of Act 46’s cost that grows after 2026.

How it was derived

  • Two sets of rules. Each year, ITEP applies 2026 law and Act 24 as enrolled to the same residents, with incomes held at 2026 levels.
  • Average times residents. The workbook shows −$812 per resident in 2031. Times about 766,000 residents (implied by the sheet), that is $622 million.
  • Income tax, residents only. Nonresidents and Act 24’s credit changes are left out.
