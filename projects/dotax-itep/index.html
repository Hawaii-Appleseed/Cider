Aligning them. DOTAX reports fiscal years and includes nonresidents;1 ITEP reports TY2031 at 2026 incomes, residents only.2 3 Matching them means differencing two DOTAX lines and mapping FY to TY — here FY = TY+1 (the alternative gives −$751M, +$104M, −$647M). Caution: nonresidents and growth both push DOTAX up, so like-for-like would put ITEP 25–30% above it.
